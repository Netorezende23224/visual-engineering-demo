Visual Engineering · WindLab

WindLab for Windows looks up addresses in Queensland and New South Wales. Review the indicative wind region, compare wind actions across building faces and export the results to PDF. The Visual Engineering subscription covers this evaluation workflow for one engineering practice.

Download the example wind report (PDF)

The example uses a fictional building. Engineers must verify the wind region, site assumptions, geometry, openings and pressure coefficients before using results on a project. Whole-shed stability and connection qualification remain under development.
