Yuruga Street, The Gap, Brisbane. Fixed educational site at the midpoint of the public road near MGA56 E 494611, N 6964541. The layers are separate datasets.

Data snapshot: https://github.com/jmogil/the-gap-vr/tree/68c0f0c6fc97fbd7b06c6d53b63fe47fbc8a0321
Building geometry (footprints.json): subset of export/buildings.geojson. © OpenStreetMap contributors and Microsoft (Global ML Building Footprints), Open Database Licence 1.0 https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . Additional outlines and available roof heights derived from Queensland Brisbane 2019 LiDAR point cloud, © State of Queensland (Department of Resources), distributed by Geoscience Australia under CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Geometry translated to local east/north metres and rounded. The subset is distributed under ODbL 1.0. Original source and mapped building type retained; personal addresses excluded. Heights recorded as null where the source only had an assumed default.
Ground profiles (profile.json): bare-earth elevations in metres AHD sampled from the Brisbane 2019 Queensland LiDAR DEM (Elevation/QldDem), © State of Queensland. Eight true-north rays sampled at 1 m intervals out to 500 m. Source service licence CC BY-SA; these adapted profiles are available under the same terms. https://spatial-img.information.qld.gov.au/arcgis/rest/services/Elevation/QldDem/ImageServer
Aerial (aerial.webp): © State of Queensland, Brisbane 2022 orthophotography (Brisbane_LGA_2022_10cm_SISP, LatestStateProgram_AllUsers). Cropped, reprojected to true north and resized. Source service licence CC BY-SA; this adapted aerial is available under the same terms. https://spatial-img.information.qld.gov.au/arcgis/rest/services/Basemaps/LatestStateProgram_AllUsers/ImageServer
The original data collection and attribution are documented at the snapshot above. Each data licence applies to its respective layer.
