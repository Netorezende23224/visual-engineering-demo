Survey basis and interpretation

Live footprints come from OpenStreetMap. The Yuruga Street extract also includes Microsoft and LiDAR-derived outlines, and measured LiDAR roof heights where available. Housing tags identify mapped residential buildings. Other buildings also act as obstacles. A footprint containing the site is excluded from neighbourhood counts. Each other building is assigned once, by its footprint centroid. Density is the count per hectare in each distance band. Height comes from a mapped height tag or a labelled estimate of 3 m per mapped floor. Missing heights remain unknown.

Terrain candidates use footprint density, height evidence and, where available, mapped open land. These are screening assumptions. Confirm mapping completeness, trees and other obstacles, recent development and upwind terrain transitions before adopting TC.

Live Terrain Tiles elevations sample eight true-north transects at 25 stations per ray. Profiles use a global elevation mosaic and may include vegetation or buildings. Local comparisons flag a possible crest when the site is within 2 m vertically of the profile high point, the high point is within 10% of the radius horizontally, and the site is at least 10 m above typical ground in the outer half on both sides. Confirm survey levels, ridge shape and upwind slope lengths before entering the topographic multiplier Mt.
