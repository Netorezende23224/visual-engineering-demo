Calculation basis and limits

Horizontal, drained backfill with active Rankine pressure, level ground and an interior post. Average, Good and Very good use trial unit weights of 17, 18 and 19 kN/m³ and friction angles of 27°, 32° and 35°. Trial clay uses 18 kN/m³ and 20°. Poor soil needs project values. A weathered-rock label requires a geotechnical assessment.

The founding stratum is a separate, assumed homogeneous undrained clay underneath each trial. The rigid, free-head Broms screen checks ultimate lateral equilibrium. It does not predict foundation movement. The depth includes clearance below the steel. Section flexure uses producer effective moduli at up to 300 MPa. Service movement is post-only, limited here to H/200.

G means lateral action from soil self-weight, Q is surcharge and W is net fence wind toward the cut. A strip surcharge uses a parallel, infinitely long loaded strip and elastic vertical stress integrated down the wall, scaled by Kₐ. LC9 is a full simultaneous comparison envelope. Cage trials use 32 MPa concrete, N500 longitudinal bars, 75 mm cover and a 10 mm nominal tie, with a pure-flexure reduction factor of 0.60. The trial cage uses two bar orientations in an unoccupied circular section with zero axial load. Steel embedment assumes opposing uniform concrete bearing blocks on the post flange.
